# Privacy Policy for Cooked Clips App

**Effective Date:** November 22, 2026

## Overview
Cooked Clips App ("we", "our", or "us") operates an automated content creation and publishing pipeline for YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any data processed by our applications.

## Information We Process
Our application interacts strictly with official platform APIs (YouTube Data API v3 and Meta Graph API) to automate video uploads, retrieve public view metrics, and manage content distribution. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal user information to third parties.

## Data Usage & Storage
- **Authentication**: API credentials and OAuth tokens are stored securely in encrypted environment variables or private GitHub repository secrets.
- **Publishing**: Video assets and public metadata (captions, tags) are uploaded directly to the authorized accounts managed by the user.
- **Third-Party Services**: We comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy and Meta Platform Terms.

## User Rights & Data Deletion
Users may revoke application access at any time through their Google Account Security Settings or Facebook Business Integrations Manager.
